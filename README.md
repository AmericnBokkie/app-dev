# My Favorite Series: Team Fortress 2

## Overview
**Team Fortress 2** is a legendary team-based multiplayer first-person shooter developed and published by **Valve**. Set in a stylized, retro-60s spy-tech world, two rival companies—**RED** (Reliable Excavation Demolition) and **BLU** (Builders League United)—hire class-based mercenaries to fight for control over objectives across various game modes.

The game is renowned for its distinct art style, humor, deep class synergies, and iconic roster of nine unique playable characters.

---

## Main Characters

### 1. The Heavy
- **Role:** Defense
- **Weapon of Choice:** Minigun ("Sasha")
- **Description:** A towering Russian giant who deals massive close-range damage and acts as the team's primary front-line tank.

### 2. The Medic
- **Role:** Support
- **Weapon of Choice:** Medigun
- **Description:** An eccentric German doctor who keeps teammates healed and can trigger invulnerability via his signature UberCharge.

### 3. The Scout
- **Role:** Offense
- **Weapon of Choice:** Scattergun
- **Description:** A fast-talking mercenary from Boston with double-jump capabilities, specializing in hit-and-run flanking tactics.

### 4. The Spy
- **Role:** Support / Infiltration
- **Weapon of Choice:** Revolver & Butterfly Knife
- **Description:** A suave French covert operative who uses disguises, cloaking devices, and instakill backstabs to dismantle enemy lines.

### 5. The Sniper
- **Role:** Support
- **Weapon of Choice:** Sniper Rifle
- **Description:** An Australian marksman who excels at long-range elimination, taking down high-priority targets from afar.
